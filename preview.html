About

BIM isn't just modelling software — it's how teams design, coordinate and deliver.

I’m Madhu Sudhan Chebrolu, a Digital Engineer at McGee in London. I hold an MSc in Construction Management with BIM from Coventry University, achieved with Distinction, and a background in Civil Engineering.

My work brings together structural drafting, BIM and software development. I build MCP integrations for Revit, Civil 3D, 3ds Max, Robot Structural Analysis and Tekla Structural Designer. I’m also developing pyRevit tools to simplify everyday Revit tasks.

What I’m working on.

Alongside project work, I connect the software I model in to AI, try it on real tasks, and turn what holds up into tools.

  1. Connect

    MCP integrations

    I’ve built Model Context Protocol integrations for five applications, so an AI assistant can read a model and act inside the software itself.

    A few of the commands each one exposes.

  2. Test

    Trial and testing

    Then I try them on the kind of tasks I meet in modelling and analysis: what they do reliably, where they fall over, and where an engineer still has to make the call.

    Still exploring how far each one can go.

  3. Build

    Tools in Revit, Civil 3D and more

    What holds up becomes a tool: pyRevit buttons in Revit and automation in Civil 3D, for the jobs that come round on every project.

    • Other repetitive modelling tasks

About

BIM isn't just modelling software — it's how teams design, coordinate and deliver.

I’m Madhu Sudhan Chebrolu, a Digital Engineer at McGee in London. I hold an MSc in Construction Management with BIM from Coventry University, achieved with Distinction, and a background in Civil Engineering.

My work brings together structural drafting, BIM and software development. I build MCP integrations for Revit, Civil 3D, 3ds Max, Robot Structural Analysis and Tekla Structural Designer. I’m also developing pyRevit tools to simplify everyday Revit tasks.
